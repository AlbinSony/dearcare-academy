Program Overview

General Duty Assistant course trains you to provide critical support to nursing staff and ensure daily patient comfort and safety in corporate hospitals, clinics, and diagnostic centers.

The General Duty Assistant (GDA) course provides comprehensive training in patient care, healthcare support, and hospital assistance. Students learn essential skills such as assisting patients with daily activities, maintaining hygiene and comfort, monitoring basic health parameters, and supporting medical and nursing staff. The program combines theoretical knowledge with practical exposure, preparing learners for entry-level roles in hospitals, clinics, nursing homes, and other healthcare facilities.
